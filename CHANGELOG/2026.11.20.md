## 2026.11.20

### Highlights

- Flow can import the whole Xero sales history as read-only invoices, with their Xero numbers, one year at a time. Each year has a dry run first and an undo after. Nothing imports until an owner or admin starts a year. (#559, #560)
- An imported invoice shows on the Invoices page in its tab (Awaiting payment, Paid, Void), marked "Imported", with its jobs. Every imported invoice has a job. (#561)
- Invoices that Flow made from Xero invoices before this release can become their Xero invoices, with their Xero numbers. A dry run shows each one first. (#562)
- Void is only for an invoice a customer saw. (#558)

### Changes

- An imported invoice is read-only: Flow never sends, voids, deletes or changes it, and never writes it to Xero. (#559)
- The Xero history import runs in batches by year, with a dry run, a follow step for later changes in Xero, and an undo for a batch. (#560)
- The Invoices page and the invoice page show imported invoices, and the jobs each one bills, also with the Xero adapter off. (#561)
- An owner or admin can change each earlier made copy into its Xero invoice. The job it billed stays billed or paid, and Flow does not draft it again. (#562)

### Fixes

- Flow voids only an invoice that reached the customer. An invoice that never reached Xero is deleted, not voided. (#558)
